DRAMA ALERT: Kanye West & Bianca Censori Divorce? Rep Shuts Down Rumors

Despite multiple reports, no legal filings or separation have occurred, and their rep confirms they are still together.

By Jordan Westbrook
Feb 15, 2025
  • Kanye West & Bianca Censori are not divorcing—despite tabloid chaos. Their rep, Milo Yiannopoulos, shut down the rumors, calling them baseless, and confirmed the couple is still together in Los Angeles.
  • The drama exploded after Bianca’s barely-there outfit at the 2025 Grammys and Kanye’s Super Bowl stunt—where he used a 15-second ad to direct people to his site selling swastika-emblazoned merch (seriously, what?).
  • Bianca might be rethinking her life choices, but for now, she’s sticking around. Kanye, meanwhile, is also dealing with a lawsuit from a former Yeezy employee, because why have one scandal when you can have five?
The Gist

Welp, here we go again—Kanye & Bianca’s divorce rumors have entered the chat—like an unkillable cockroach or an NSYNC reunion rumor that never actually happens. Look, the tabloids swore this time was for real: The Daily Mail and TMZ were all “OMG IT’S OVER”, acting like they had insider knowledge straight from Bianca’s group chat. But plot twist: THEY’RE STILL TOGETHER. Or so says Milo Yiannopoulos, their forever-exhausted rep who probably has a pre-written email template just for this.

“Ye and Bianca are in Los Angeles, about to enjoy Valentine’s Day together. Announcements about their private life will come from them directly, not unsourced rumor in the tabloid press,” Yiannopoulos told The Hollywood Reporter.

The Tabloid Industrial Complex—Just Making Stuff Up for Sport

So where did this latest round of “They’re Done” come from?? Oh, you know—just the usual suspects (Daily Mail, TMZ, your aunt on Facebook). They claimed Kanye and Bianca had “split” and were calling their lawyers because, IDK, maybe they got bored???

AND THEN—because no rich-person gossip is complete without money drama—there were reports saying Kanye was gonna Venmo her $5 million as a divorce settlement. ($5M?? Bro, that’s like three moderately overpriced LA houses, calm down.)

This was all supposedly coming from a “source close to Kanye”—which, let’s be honest, could be ANYONE. The guy probably has a raccoon in his backyard who knows more about his life than some of these “insiders.”

Are They Actually Okay Though???

Right now, no divorce papers have been filed. Next week? Who knows. Next month? LOL, good luck predicting that.

Bianca’s been dealing with A LOT lately. You’d think being married to Kanye is already enough chaos for one lifetime, but nope—she also had to survive The Great Grammy Outfit Meltdown of 2025.

That Time Bianca Was Too Naked for the Grammys

So if you somehow missed it—because maybe you were busy doing normal things like eating guacomole and minding your business—Bianca showed up at the 2025 Grammy Awards in a barely there dress that was essentially air and good intentions. The internet LOST ITS MIND.

People were either SCREAMING about indecency or applauding her for breaking the simulation—no in-between. Meanwhile, Kanye was LOVING IT, hyping up his wife’s “hot” figure and, in peak Kanye energy, deciding that they won the Grammys purely based on Google search volume that night.

Sir… that’s not how awards work.

And Then Things Got Way Worse

Because Kanye—being Kanye—couldn’t just sit back and enjoy the wild ride of public outrage. No, he had to go FULL KANYE, which in 2025 means:

  1. Going on another antisemitic rant
  2. Selling swastika-emblazoned t-shirts on his Yeezy website
  3. Dropping a Super Bowl ad that led people straight to those disaster shirts

…So yeah. This might be the actual reason people thought Bianca was ready to dip.

And honestly? Can you blame her???

Bianca: Trapped or Just Really Committed to the Bit???

This is where the internet conspiracy machine kicks in. Some people are CONVINCED that Kanye is controlling Bianca’s entire existence, like a Sims character with free will turned OFF.

But Milo Yiannopoulos was NOT here for that narrative, shutting it down in the most exhausted tone possible:

“Bianca has full control over her fashion choices.”

Okay then.

But DOES SHE THO? Because girl has been looking progressively more exhausted in recent paparazzi pics. Like, that’s not just “married to a famous guy” tired. That’s “married to a guy who keeps making me part of a new global controversy every other week” tired.

Also, Kanye Is Getting Sued (Because of Course He Is)

Oh yeah—while all this marriage madness is going down, Kanye is ALSO getting sued by a former Yeezy employee for discrimination and harassment. Because obviously, he needed more problems.

So…???

For now, the official word is: NO DIVORCE. But would anyone be surprised if tomorrow Bianca just peaced out and moved to an undisclosed tropical island to sell luxury matcha on Instagram?

Exactly.

Until then, Kanye’s probably gonna keep doing Kanye things, Bianca’s gonna keep being a mystery, and the tabloids will NEVER shut up about it.
